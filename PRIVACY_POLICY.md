# Privacy Policy for Dining Philosophers Café

**Last updated:** November 1, 2026

Dining Philosophers Café ("the App") is an educational simulation tool designed to demonstrate classical computer science synchronization problems, deadlocks, and concurrency algorithms.

### 1. Information Collection and Use
Dining Philosophers Café does **not** collect, store, transmit, or share any personally identifiable information (PII). 

- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide any personal details to use the App.
- **No Analytics or Trackers:** The App does not contain third-party tracking libraries, advertising SDKs, or behavioral telemetry.
- **No Background Telemetry:** The App does not transmit diagnostics or usage statistics to external servers.

### 2. Device Storage & Local Preferences
The App stores only one local preference directly on your device via standard local storage:
- **Theme preference:** Remembers your choice between Light Mode and Dark Mode.
This data is stored solely on your device and is never transmitted over the network.

### 3. Permissions
The App does not request any sensitive permissions:
- No location access
- No camera or microphone access
- No contacts or phone state access
- No external file system access

### 4. Children's Privacy
Because the App does not collect any personal data, it complies with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) requirements. The App is safe for learners, students, and users of all ages.

### 5. Third-Party Services
The App does not integrate with any third-party advertising, analytics, or payment networks.

### 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
If we update this Privacy Policy in the future, the changes will be posted here with an updated effective date.

### 7. Contact Us
If you have any questions or feedback regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact the developer via the GitHub repository or developer contact page.
